## Welcome!

I specialize in designing Monte Carlo variance reduction techniques and HPC algorithms for physical simulations and implementing them in Fortran and C++. My [experience](https://vitmog.github.io/brand-hist/) involves nuclear engineering applications of such techniques to industrial [shielding evaluations](https://vitmog.github.io/shielding-evaluations/) for [safety analysis](https://vitmog.github.io/bio/#industrial-activity-2011-2021). The obtained results show that Monte Carlo shielding calculations with highly detailed dose rate mapping can be performed without requiring significant computational resources.

|:--:|
| Several hours (around 3--10) long neutron and gamma dose rate computation [results](https://vitmog.github.io/shielding-evaluations/#calculational-results) obtained on a dual-core **Intel Core i3-5005U CPU @ 2.00GHz** based laptop |

I am open to collaborations, primarily in the field of adoption and implementation of existing variance reduction techniques (see [preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16781416)) and the design of new _ad hoc_ ones for specific Monte Carlo codes for both neutral and charged particle transport, but I am also interested in developing new fields, such as path-tracing rendering.
